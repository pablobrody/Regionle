How to play

  1. A random region (state, province, voivodeship…) of a mystery country appears on the map.
  2. Type your guess for the country and hit Guess.
  3. Wrong? A new region is revealed and you see how far your guess was from the correct answer.
  4. Keep guessing until you find it. Fewer regions = better score!

💡 Toggle Show names to reveal region labels as a hint.
